📋 Detailed Specifications & Behavior

Click each section to inspect its concrete UX rules and acceptance criteria:

1. Single-Page Dashboard Layout ▼
  • Header: ExamSprint branding with dark theme and purple/cyan glow.
  • Summary Counters: Total Subjects, Total Topics, Completed Topics, and High Priority Topics.
  • Split View (Desktop): Left column (Input Form) + Right column (Ranked Study Queue). Stacks vertically on mobile screens.
  • Bottom Section: Collapsible "Completed Topics" archive with toggle count.
2. Dynamic Priority Engine & Cards ▼

Every topic card calculates a Priority Score (1–100) and displays a plain-English reason:

HIGH (70–100) Red badge — e.g., "Exam in 2 days and Low confidence. Revise this topic first."
MEDIUM (40–69) Orange/Yellow badge — e.g., "Exam in 7 days with Medium confidence. Review this topic soon."
LOW (1–39) Green badge — e.g., "Exam in 18 days with High confidence. Keep fresh with light revision."
  • In-place Confidence: Students can change confidence directly on the card to watch the score recalculate and reorder without refreshing.
  • Edit & Delete: Dedicated Edit modal and Delete button with confirmation modal.
3. Form Validation & Edge Cases ▼
  • Required Field Validation: Blank Subject or Topic shows clear inline red error (e.g. "Please enter a topic name.").
  • Past Date Prevention: Exam dates prior to today are blocked with "Exam date cannot be in the past. Please select today or a future date." Today is allowed.
  • State Retention: Preserves user inputs on error so nothing has to be retyped.
  • Whitespace Trimming: Removes leading and trailing whitespace.
4. Completed Archive & Progress ▼
  • Marking complete moves the card to the collapsible Completed section.
  • Card is styled with green checkmark, faded background, and strikethrough text.
  • Increments the "Completed Topics" summary counter.
  • Includes an "Undo" / "Mark as Incomplete" button that restores the card to the active queue with live recalculated ranking.
