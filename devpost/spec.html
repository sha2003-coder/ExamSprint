⚡ Dynamic Priority Score Formula

Pure JavaScript formula calculating score from 10 to 95:

Factor Condition Points Awarded Rationale
Urgency Points
(Max 65)		 0 to 2 days left 65 pts Imminent exam — highest urgency
3 to 7 days left 45 pts Exam this week
8 to 14 days left 25 pts Exam next week
15+ days left 10 pts Upcoming with comfortable buffer
Confidence Points
(Max 30)		 Low Confidence 30 pts Struggling; needs urgent revision

High Confidence 0 pts Prepared; low revision urgency

📂 Technical Blueprint Modules

Component Hierarchy (7 Components + 1 Utility) ▼
  • App.jsx: Central state manager, localStorage sync, modal controller.
  • Navbar.jsx: ExamSprint header, logo, and subtitle.
  • SummaryCards.jsx: 4 metric cards (Total Subjects, Total Topics, High Priority Topics, Completed Topics).
  • TopicForm.jsx: Form with inline validation (empty check, past-date prevention).
  • StudyQueue.jsx: Sorted priority list with empty state fallback.
  • TopicCard.jsx: Topic item with score badge, in-place confidence selector, and action buttons.
  • CompletedArchive.jsx: Collapsible drawer for completed topics with Undo button.
  • ConfirmModal.jsx: Delete confirmation dialog.
  • priorityEngine.js: Pure JS date diff and scoring algorithm.

Testing & Verification Checklist (10 Criteria) ▼
  1. Empty State: 4 metric cards start at 0, welcoming prompt displayed.
  2. Input Validation: Rejects blank inputs with inline errors, blocks past dates, accepts today.
  3. Scoring Math: Urgency (65/45/25/10) + Confidence (30/15/0) accurately totals 10–95.
  4. Auto-Sorting: Queue renders cards sorted strictly descending by Priority Score.
  5. In-Place Updates: Toggling card confidence recalculates score and moves card immediately.
  6. Editing: Modify topic, subject, or date smoothly via Edit action.
  7. Delete Confirmation: Confirm modal prevents accidental deletion; Cancel retains card.
  8. Archive & Undo: Completed topics move to collapsible archive; Undo restores to active queue.
  9. Past Due Handling: Previously saved cards with expired dates display "PAST DUE" warning badge.
  10. Persistence & Responsive: Survives browser reload (F5) via localStorage; responsive desktop & mobile.
